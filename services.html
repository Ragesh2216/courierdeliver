MODAL SPECIFICATIONS

Detailed breakdown & operational profiles

Road freight

The workhorse of local delivery. Road freight connects homes, shops and warehouses directly, handling anything from a single parcel to a full pallet. Daily schedules and door-to-door collection make it the most adaptable option.

Ocean freight

Built for volume. Containers move large, heavy or non-urgent cargo across oceans at the lowest cost per unit. Choose it when lead time is measured in weeks and the cargo travels well.

Air freight

The fastest way across borders. Air freight suits high-value, urgent or lightweight shipments — documents, medical supplies, electronics — where speed matters more than the price per kilo.

Rail transport

A dependable middle path. Rail carries steady, high-volume loads along fixed inland corridors, offering predictable transit times with a lighter footprint than road over long distances.

Drone delivery

Point to point for the small and urgent. Drones bridge short distances for lightweight parcels where a vehicle would add delay or a road simply does not exist, subject to local approvals.

Last mile

The final handoff. The journey ends at the doorstep with planned routes, delivery updates and a personal touch that completes the trip — whichever mode carried the parcel there.
